Fit for life

Physical fitness is important for longevity because it keeps your heart healthy, strengthens muscles and bones, and reduces the risk of chronic diseases. Staying active helps maintain mobility, independence, and a better quality of life as we age.

Key components of fitness

Achieving overall health requires balancing multiple types of exercise throughout your weekly routine.

Essential Elements

  • Cardiovascular Endurance (Running, Swimming, Walking)
  • Strength Training (Weightlifting, bodyweight exercises)
  • Flexibility and mobility (Stretching, Yoga)
  • Balance and Stability core work

Daily habits for longevity

Consistency is key when building a lasting lifestyle change. Start with small, daily movements and build up gradually over time.
